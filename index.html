OVERVIEW

Perception-aware hybrid extremum seeking

The objective is to steer the robot toward the maximizer set AJ\mathcal A_JAJ​ of an unknown scalar field J while maintaining the admissible obstacle-free region. The controller uses online field measurements at the physical robot position and a calibrated position estimate supplied by learned visual perception; it does not require a field model or gradient measurements.

Controller-aware Adaptive Perception (CAP) adjusts visual processing according to the localization accuracy needed along the trajectory. A local expert is evaluated first. The controller’s tolerance, presence probability, and miss history determine whether to invoke a context or full-frame expert.

The holonomic design uses a periodic exploration signal and obstacle-aware covering regions. The nonholonomic design uses obstacle-relative manifold coordinates and oscillatory inputs whose averaged effect is characterized by Lie brackets. Hysteretic switching selects among mode-dependent localization functions. The manuscript establishes practical stability and obstacle clearance under its stated assumptions and parameter-selection conditions.

During flow, q is constant. At a jump, only q changes; the continuous plant and oscillator states are preserved. Selecting a minimizing mode resets the switching gap to zero. The threshold δi\delta_iδi​ is chosen below the strict synergistic gap required by the stability construction. At equality, the hybrid model permits either flow or a jump.

The mode q identifies the active localization function. It does not identify a perception expert or a fixed direction of motion. A successful trajectory may remain in one mode if its switching condition is never met.
